# Privacy Policy for Gaga Pit Showdown

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

Apps by Dan ("we," "us," or "our") provides the mobile game **Gaga Pit
Showdown**, Android package `com.appsbydan.gagapitshowdown` (the "Game"). This
Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles information.

## Summary

Gaga Pit Showdown is designed to work without an account or online service. The
Game does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. It
does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not request access
to sensitive Android permissions.

## Information stored on the device

The Game stores the following information only in its private storage on the
player's device:

- player-created character names;
- character appearance and clothing selections;
- the selected home city; and
- game, tour, league, unlock, and settings progress.

This information is used only to provide save-game and customization features.
It is not sent to Apps by Dan or any third party. A character name is a local
game label; players should avoid entering sensitive personal information.

## Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect or transmit:

- real names, email addresses, postal addresses, or telephone numbers;
- account credentials or authentication information;
- device or advertising identifiers;
- location, contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone data;
- gameplay analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports;
- payment or financial information; or
- messages, user-generated content, or information from other users.

The current Android release does not request internet access or other sensitive
Android permissions. The Game has no online multiplayer, cloud save, social
login, advertising, or in-app purchasing system.

## External links

The Game includes an optional **Support the Dev** button. If selected, it asks
the device to open `https://buymeacoffee.com/appsbydan` in the user's external
web browser. The website is not operated by the Game, and any information a
user chooses to provide there is governed by that website's own privacy policy
and terms. The Game does not receive browser activity or information entered on
that website.

Google Play and the device operating system may independently process
information when a user downloads, installs, purchases, updates, or reviews the
Game. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies
and is outside the Game's control.

## Children's privacy

The Game may appeal to children and families. It does not knowingly collect,
use, or disclose personal information from children or any other players. It
contains no targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, chat, or public sharing
features. Player-created names and character settings remain only on the local
device.

If a parent or guardian believes a child entered personal information as a
local character name, that name can be edited or removed within the Game. All
locally stored Game information can also be removed by clearing the Game's app
data or uninstalling it.

## Data retention and deletion

Local save information remains on the device until the player changes it,
clears the Game's app data, or uninstalls the Game. Because the information is
not transmitted to us, we cannot access, recover, or delete it remotely.

## Data security and international transfers

Save information is kept in the Game's private local app storage using the
protections provided by Android. No system can guarantee absolute security,
but the Game reduces privacy risk by not collecting or transmitting player
information. Because the Game does not send player information to us, we do not
transfer such information internationally.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or legal obligations
change. The revised policy will identify a new "Last updated" date. If a future
version adds online services or data collection, this policy and the Google Play
Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.
